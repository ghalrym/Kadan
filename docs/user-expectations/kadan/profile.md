# Your profile

Kadan gives the person using it a profile: a name and a pet. Kadan shows the profile at the bottom of the sidebar; Tsuchi stores it.

- Choose your profile at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Your profile**. Change your name and choose **Save**, or choose **Edit pet** to pick a pet.
- Your pet comes from the same pet library as your agent's pets. **Edit pet** lets you create, import, preview and choose a finished pet exactly as described in [Pets](pets.md), and **Use default pet** returns to Kadan's default pet. Choosing a pet for yourself does not change your agent's pet.
- Your name and pet appear on your [Group Chat](group-chat.md) messages. Agents see your name on new messages so they know who is talking. Messages you sent before a rename keep the name they were sent with for agents, while Kadan shows your current name and pet on all of them.
- Only finished pets can be chosen. If your pet is later trashed or can no longer be loaded, Kadan shows the default pet in its place.
- If someone else changed the profile since you opened it, saving reports a problem and offers **Reload profile** before you try again.
